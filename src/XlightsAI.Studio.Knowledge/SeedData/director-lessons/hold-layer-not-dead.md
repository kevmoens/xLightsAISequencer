id: hold-layer-not-dead
applies: always
priority: 15
---
A "hold"-motion layer is a steady state, never a dead one. Confirmed against a real exported show: a layer using the plain "On" effect, reused across several consecutive sections for the same subject/group, renders as one long, visually unbroken, boring block -- exactly as lifeless as the bare negative-space problem above, just on the opposite (always-on) extreme. "On" has no animation of its own, so reserve it for brief transitional moments (well under a bar) -- never as the sole, long-duration content of a "hold" layer or a Look reused across many consecutive sections. For an actual held/steady look, pick an effect with its own real per-frame animation instead (Color Wash, Twinkle, SingleStrand, Bars, ...) -- it still reads as calm and steady at a low duty cycle, but it is never a single dead frame stretched over a minute.
