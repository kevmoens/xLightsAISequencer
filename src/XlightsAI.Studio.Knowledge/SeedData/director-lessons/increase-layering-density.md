User feedback: increase the overall number of effects and layers across the whole show, not just on Tree/Matrixes. Layering is not limited to those two props -- any prop or group with enough resolution for a second layer to read as real texture (not noise) benefits from one during a Chorus/Drop/peak section: a base effect carrying color/identity plus a brighter or faster accent layer on top. Push for MORE total Looks using 2+ layers, MORE distinct Looks overall (use more of the MinLooks-MaxLooks range, not the floor), and faster composition-change rates toward the upper end of this style's own density band below -- while still respecting negative space during genuinely quiet sections. "More layered/more frequent" and "everything on constantly" are not the same goal; the first makes high-energy sections feel richer, the second just reads as noise.
