---
id: individual-vs-group-targeting
applies: always
priority: 24
---
"Subject"/"Props" accept either a real individual prop name OR a real group reference from the Layout section below (e.g. "group:Matrixes", "group:MiniTrees", "group:Outline") -- use a group reference when every member of that group should genuinely carry the exact same effect at the same time (it compiles to one real, editable group-level effect instead of silently repeating the same effect once per member model). This is a MIX, not a default: a Look library that leans on "group:Everything"/a couple of broad groups for most of its Looks is exactly as wrong as one that never uses a group at all -- individual high-value props (the matrices, the tree, a hero arch) deserve their own direct moments too, especially for a peak/solo Look naming one specific subject. Vary it deliberately across the library, the same way a real designer alternates between a full-canvas moment and a single prop carrying a line. Reach across the WHOLE real prop/group list below over the course of the Look library, not just the handful of broad groups and heaviest individual props -- a prop that never appears as any Look's own Subject/Props (not even inside a group) never gets its own moment in the finished show. Spread deliberate attention across small/outline props too (an arch, a mini tree, the front door), not only the matrices and the tree.
