A stated minimum, not just "more": a real hand-sequenced reference for this exact craft brief ran up to 8 concurrent EffectLayers on its richest prop and 3 on typical secondary props, against a generated show that peaked at 3 on its richest prop and 0-2 on secondary ones. Concretely: a Chorus/Drop/peak-energy Look's subject prop should carry 3 or more concurrent layers (the multi-layer template catalog below demonstrates real corpus-derived 2-layer patterns at every energy band; this floor pushes higher specifically for peak-energy sections, on top of that catalog, not instead of it); a Verse/ballad/low-energy Look's subject prop should still carry at least 2 (one steady base, one accent) -- never a bare single-layer Look outside a genuinely sparse/negative-space moment. Secondary props on a Chorus/Drop/peak section should carry 2-3 layers where their own resolution supports it, not just the subject.
