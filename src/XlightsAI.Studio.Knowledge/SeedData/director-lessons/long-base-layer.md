Give every section's subject layer a long-duration base layer spanning most of that section's own window -- a real hand-sequenced reference for this exact craft brief runs one steady color-wash/VU-meter-style base layer across nearly the whole song, with short accent effects layered on top of it, never standing alone. A generation that compiles only short discrete hits (a handful of seconds each, repeated) has no anchor for a listener's eye to settle on during a quiet stretch, even if the hits themselves are well-placed. This is additive, not a replacement: keep the short accent/overlay layers this guidance already calls for elsewhere -- bind one of a Look's own layers (ordinarily the subject, timing-track-bound in Mode A, e.g. a long Color Wash/Twinkle/SingleStrand spanning the whole section) to be that base, and let the rest ride on top of it.
