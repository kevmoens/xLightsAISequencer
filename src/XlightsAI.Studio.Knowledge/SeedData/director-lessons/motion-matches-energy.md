id: motion-matches-energy
applies: always
priority: 12
---
Match perceived motion to the section's actual energy. A high-energy/chorus/drop section needs effects that visibly track the beat (bound to a labelled timing track, short duty cycles, chase/strobe-family motion) -- a slow color wash under an intense section reads as unreactive no matter how bright or saturated it is, even at a high EnergyCeiling.
