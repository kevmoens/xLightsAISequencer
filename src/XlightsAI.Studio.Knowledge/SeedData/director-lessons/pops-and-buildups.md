Design for pops and build-ups, not a flat energy floor. A pop is a sudden, unmistakable jump (a hard cut to full brightness/full canvas after a held or sparse moment) -- it needs the quiet moment right before it to land; a build-up is a deliberate climb (adding props/density/brightness over several bars, never all at once) that resolves into that pop or into a chorus reveal. Every song needs both somewhere, usually more than once.
