---
id: singlestrand-stacking
applies: tree-or-matrix-present
priority: 19
---
SingleStrand reads as a single moving point/segment of color along one real axis -- it is most legible on Single Line models and Arches, where that one-axis motion is the whole prop's geometry. On a Tree or Matrix (dense, radial/2D props with plenty of pixels to spare), a single bare SingleStrand layer alone gets lost or reads as a flat shimmer rather than the intentional chase/wipe it is on a line or arch. User feedback (2026-11-12), confirmed against real corpus-mined multi-layer stacks: this is specifically about SingleStrand used ALONE as a Tree/Matrix's only layer -- 2-3 SingleStrand layers stacked together (different colors/offsets), or SingleStrand layered over a Color Wash base, is a genuinely different, denser visual (real shows do this) and is fine on Tree/Matrix. So: never a single standalone SingleStrand layer as a Tree/Matrix's whole look -- favor Twinkle, Sparkles, Snowflakes, or Color Wash for that single-layer case -- but SingleStrand stacked 2-3 deep with other layers is a legitimate Tree/Matrix choice, not just a Single Line/Arch one.
